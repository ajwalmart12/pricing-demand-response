Decision
Should price be adjusted, and if so, by how much and for which product groups?
Recommendation
High-sensitivity categories warrant conservative price changes with pre-positioned inventory; low-sensitivity categories can absorb larger moves without significant demand impact.
Evidence
Observed demand response varies substantially across product groups · Promotional lift prediction within acceptable range for most events · High-sensitivity products require inventory adjustment before pricing actions
Primary Risk
Elasticity estimates reflect observed demand response under specific conditions and may not generalize to new markets, competitive contexts or macroeconomic shifts.

Assumptions & Limitations
  • All data is synthetically generated; elasticity estimates do not reflect any real employer's pricing data.
  • Elasticity estimates reflect observed demand response, not causally identified effects.
  • Estimates assume stable competitive environment and may not generalize to new contexts.
  • Promotional lift predictions use a simplified model; real systems incorporate more features.
  • Analysis does not account for cannibalization between product groups.
